## Mathematical and scope audit

The forcing framework is an explicitly defined finite-rank game, with a named winning colour, an upper bound in plies, zero-ply opponent mate, a choosing move for the winner and a nonempty all-replies obligation for the defender. It proves soundness and finite-branching closure; it does not claim equivalence to a separately represented arbitrary eventual-winning policy or complete FIDE clock/history outcomes.

The confinement theorem is an all-defences preservation statement for exact KRK material, either colour, any flags, with the lone king to move. The rook is king-protected and strictly above its king, with the lone king still higher. Every legal reply preserves the separating rank. It asserts neither a reply's existence nor strict progress. Raising the rook while fixing its king below the old rank loses immediate protection; that recorded obstruction remains the next strategy's obligation.

The fixed examples were compiled and their statements checked: optional rook capture prevents forcing despite liveness; zero-/one-ply mate; wrong winning colour; protected-rank stalemate; a king on the rook rank blocking its ray. They are supporting witnesses, not general enumeration proofs. No new discovery, tablebase lookup, engine oracle or independent external audit is claimed in this freeze session.
