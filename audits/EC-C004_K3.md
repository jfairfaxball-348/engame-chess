## 1. What K3 claims
- **G2 closed.** Suppose `Legal p q` for *any* kernel position `p` (any material, any flags). If `q` holds exactly two kings, or exactly K+B vs K in either orientation, then `q` is dead in the kernel:
  - `dead_of_legal_into_KK`
  - `dead_of_legal_into_KBK`
- **Arisen forms.** Every arisen K vs K / K+B vs K position (Art. 3.10.3: reachable from `initial`) is dead:
  - `arisen_KK_dead`
  - `arisen_KBK_dead`
- **G3 made explicit.** Art. 5.2.2 is the named definition `Rule5_2_2` (*a normative encoding, not a theorem*). Under it, both classes are drawn immediately:
  - `kk_drawn_by_rule`
  - `kbk_drawn_by_rule`

## 2. How (proof structure)
1. **Flag irrelevance** (`Flags.lean`). On an `NRP` board (no rooks, no pawns):
   - `isPseudo` is independent of castling rights and the en-passant target. Castling needs a rook (`canCastle_false_of_noRook`), and the pawn branch is unreachable.
   - `play` is a plain "move the piece" update (`play_nrp`). There is no promotion, no castling branch (`not_castle_of_step`), no en-passant capture and no new target.
   - `NRP` is preserved by legal moves (`nrp_of_legal`).
   - Hence `legal_normFlags_iff`. With the generic `dead_transfer` (K2), this gives `DeadK (normFlags q) ↔ DeadK q`.
   - **This is proved, not assumed.** The flags appear only in `canCastle`, `pawnMoveOK` and `play`'s e.p./castling branches, and each one is shown inert.
2. **Last-move legality** (3.9.2, `legal_mover_safe`). The side that just moved is not in check in `q`. For a two-king board this means the kings are not adjacent, i.e. `Admissible`. For a K+B vs K board it means the side not to move is not in check, i.e. `LKBK`.
3. **Normalisation is the K2 embedding.**
   - `normFlags q = ofKK ⟨w, b, q.turn⟩`, or `= ofKBK ⟨σ, sk, bs, wk, side⟩`.
   - Then K2's `kernel_dead_KK` / `kernel_dead_KBK` apply.
4. **Arisen forms.** `cases` on `ReachK initial q`:
   - the `refl` case is impossible, because `initial` has a pawn on a2 (`initial_a2`, `initial_not_KK`, `initial_not_KBK`);
   - the `tail` case is step 1–3 above.

## 3. Hostile checks
1. **Non-vacuity of G2 with larger material.** `capture_into_KK_example`: from White Kc1, Black Qd2 (checking), Black Kh8, the move Kxd2 is kernel-legal (`decide`). The predecessor holds a queen, the result `IsKK` holds (checked square by square over all 64 squares by `decide`), and it is proved dead.
2. **No history invariant smuggled in.** The theorems quantify over all `p`; nothing about how `p` arose is assumed. In particular, a castling right left set without its rook is harmless, by flag irrelevance.
3. **Material predicates are exact.** `IsKK q w b` requires `q.board = kkBoard w b`, i.e. exactly those two kings and nothing else. `IsKBK` likewise.
4. **Both orientations.** `IsKBK` has a free strong-side colour `σ`. The side to move is recovered by `exists_side`, and the mover by `sideColour_opp`.
5. **`Rule5_2_2` fidelity.**
   - "Has arisen" is `Arisen`.
   - "Neither player can checkmate … with any series of legal moves" is `DeadK`, which says no reachable `MatedK`. A position where the side to move is in check with no legal move is exactly "a player has checkmated the opponent".
   - "Drawn immediately" is `r = .draw`.
   - The 4.2–4.7 proviso is out of scope and stated as such.
6. **Open item, stated honestly.** A general "exactly one king per colour" invariant of `Arisen` positions is *not* proved. It is not needed: the endgame theorems use only `q`'s own board and the legality of the last move. It is recorded in `docs/RULES_KERNEL.md`.
7. **Trusted base.**
   - Axioms ⊆ `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` for all 14 K3 declarations; several use fewer.
   - No `native_decide`.
   - `decide` is used only on closed concrete facts: `initial_a2`, the example position, and the 64-square board check.
8. **Frozen and earlier modules untouched.** The `KK`, `KBK`, K1 and K2 hashes are unchanged (see the closeout).
