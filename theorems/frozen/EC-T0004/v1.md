# EC-T0004 v1 — King and bishop versus king is dead in the general FIDE rules kernel (either orientation; any legal arrival; arisen positions)

**Status:** FROZEN (session S010, 2026-10-16). Source candidate: **EC-C006**. **Strengthens EC-T0002** (see below).
**Proof-source commit:** `573c96a527d20f0f619a431a132a0894503c44f8` (S009). This manifest is in a later commit and does not cite its own SHA.
**Attribution:** classical. Nothing here is claimed as novel. The contribution is a quantifier-correct, machine-checked derivation from a single, auditable encoding of the FIDE 2023 Laws.

## Statement
**Scope.** Orthodox chess on the 8×8 board under the general rules kernel `formal/EndgameChess/Rules/`. This is a Lean encoding of FIDE Laws of Chess effective 1 January 2023 (https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/E012023), Articles 1.2, 1.4.1, 2.1–3.10 and 5.2.2. The material is exactly king + bishop against a lone king, either colour as the strong side.

**Theorem (prose).** Let `q` be a kernel position holding exactly a king and a bishop of colour `σ` and a lone king of the other colour (either orientation), with any flags. If `q` was produced by a legal move from **any** position (any material, e.g. a capture of the last other piece), then `q` is dead. In particular, every arisen K+B vs K position is dead. Under `Rule5_2_2` it is drawn immediately.

This is a **no-possible-mate** statement, which is stronger than "no forced mate". It is distinct from the repetition, 50-move and 75-move rules.

**Theorem (Lean, verbatim output at the proof commit):**
```
@EndgameChess.Rules.dead_of_legal_into_KBK : ∀ {p q : EndgameChess.Rules.Position} {σ : EndgameChess.KK.Colour}
  {sk bs wk : EndgameChess.KK.Square},
  EndgameChess.Rules.Legal p q → EndgameChess.Rules.IsKBK q σ sk bs wk → EndgameChess.Rules.DeadK q
@EndgameChess.Rules.arisen_KBK_dead : ∀ {q : EndgameChess.Rules.Position} {σ : EndgameChess.KK.Colour}
  {sk bs wk : EndgameChess.KK.Square},
  EndgameChess.Rules.Arisen q → EndgameChess.Rules.IsKBK q σ sk bs wk → EndgameChess.Rules.DeadK q
```
**Rule corollary (Lean, verbatim).** `Rule5_2_2` is a **named normative encoding of Art. 5.2.2, not a theorem**:
```
def EndgameChess.Rules.Rule5_2_2 : EndgameChess.Rules.Position → EndgameChess.Rules.GameResult → Prop :=
fun q r => EndgameChess.Rules.Arisen q ∧ EndgameChess.Rules.DeadK q ∧ r = EndgameChess.Rules.GameResult.draw
@EndgameChess.Rules.kbk_drawn_by_rule : ∀ {q : EndgameChess.Rules.Position} {σ : EndgameChess.KK.Colour}
  {sk bs wk : EndgameChess.KK.Square},
  EndgameChess.Rules.Arisen q →
    EndgameChess.Rules.IsKBK q σ sk bs wk → EndgameChess.Rules.Rule5_2_2 q EndgameChess.Rules.GameResult.draw
```

## Strengthens EC-T0002
EC-T0002 was proved over a bespoke model and frozen with three prose-only trust gaps. This record closes or isolates them:
- **G1 (move-set fidelity) — closed.** S008 (K2) proved that the bespoke relation agrees *exactly* with the general kernel's `Legal` on its material class: `legal_ofKBK_iff` (all four K+B vs K move kinds, including the capture of the bishop into K vs K), `deadK_ofKBK_iff`, `kernel_dead_KBK` (S008, proof `077572528c20bd8fa9c9e1bb06cd35f9dd4ae4bd`).
- **G2 (positions arising by capture from larger material) — closed.** S009 (K3) proved the result for a position produced by **any** legal move from **any** position (`dead_of_legal_into_*`), with no history invariant needed. Castling and en-passant flags are provably irrelevant on boards with no rooks and no pawns (`deadK_normFlags_iff`). A concrete witness is `capture_into_KK_example` (Kxd2 takes a queen, leaving K vs K).
- **G3 (the Art. 5.2.2 rule step) — explicit.** It is now the named definition `Rule5_2_2`, a rule encoding rather than a theorem.

EC-T0002 itself is unchanged and remains valid as stated.
