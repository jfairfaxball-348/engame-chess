## Exact scope and semantic boundary

For either strong colour σ and arbitrary flags, exact IsKRK q σ S R W, q.turn=σ, and existence of an incoming Legal move imply ForceMate σ q. The stronger geometric theorem replaces incoming Legal by inCheck q σ.opp=false and needs no predecessor. Finite setup covers off-rank retreat with axis switch, same-rank king retreat, protected back-rank entry and preparation. No assumed winning continuation remains. For exact weak-turn KRK, ForceMate σ q iff MatedK q OR (a nonempty legal reply set AND EVERY legal reply avoids KK). Equivalently it is MatedK q OR (SafeRook S R W AND a legal reply exists). Zero-ply mate is included; stalemate and ANY legal KK capture are excluded. Every setup/reply quantifier is covered. Fixed back-rank, black flagged file-axis, and unsafe-capture controls remain supplementary. This completes the intended EC-C011 clock-free target; it does not give FIDE WDL with clocks/repetition or numerical DTM/DTZ.

Coordinates are Fin 8. Both named strong colours and arbitrary flags are quantified except where source noRights is explicitly required. Matches fixes exact board/material and turn; Legal is the general FIDE 2023 legal-move kernel. Quantifier order and all subsidiary hypotheses are authoritative in the verbatim exact-source statements below; no global initial-position reachability is added.

ForceMate is the unchanged EC-T0008 clock-free finite-rank named-colour semantics: actual opponent mate is terminal; strong choices are existential; defending legal replies are nonempty and universal; deadness stops play. A finite rank is not an optimal numeric distance. No halfmove clock, repetition, 50/75-move outcome, DTZ, complete FIDE WDL, seven-piece parity or initial-position conclusion is asserted.
