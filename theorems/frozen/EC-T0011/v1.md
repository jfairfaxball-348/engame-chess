# EC-T0011 v1 — Opposed-kings four-ply checking contraction against every defence

**Status:** FROZEN (S027, 2026-10-19). Source candidate: EC-C013.
**Representative:** `EndgameChess.Rules.krk_opposition_four_ply`.

## Exact statement and scope

Let q be exact KRK (`IsKRK q σ S R W`), **strong to move**, on the 8×8 board. The entry hypotheses are conspicuous:

- `Opposed S W := S.file = W.file ∧ W.rank = S.rank + 2`;
- `4 ≤ absDiff R.file W.file` (**four-file rook margin**);
- `R.rank ≠ W.rank` (**rook off the lone king's rank**).

Either strong colour and arbitrary flags are included. There is no global reachability hypothesis. The definitions frozen with the source include `Opposed` and `checkRook R W := (R.file, W.rank)`. `Matches` fixes exact material, board and turn; the kernel's `Legal` is the actual legal transition relation, not merely a cooperative step surrogate.

`krk_opposition_four_ply` chooses a legal checking rook move to C=`checkRook R W`. Either the result satisfies `MateGoal σ`, or it has a nonempty legal reply set and **every** first reply yields some lone-king T. A chosen king-up move S→S′ (same file, one rank up) is legal and has a nonempty second reply set; **every** second reply yields a strong-turn KRK state with `DistantBarrier S′ C V` and `7 - V.rank < 7 - W.rank`. The barrier definition is exactly `S′.rank < C.rank < V.rank ∧ 2 ≤ dInf C V`. The alternating quantifiers in the verbatim statement below are part of the frozen claim.

`krk_opposition_check` separately records that all first replies rise exactly one rank, move at most one file and leave a three-file margin. `krk_follow_check` is the second half, with its exact first-reply geometry as hypotheses. `krk_opposition_edge_force` adds `W.rank = 7` and proves `ForceMate σ q`: an actual mate-in-one family. `MateGoal σ q := q.turn = σ.opp ∧ MatedK q`; `ForceMate σ q := ∃ n, MateWithin σ n q`, the frozen clock-free/repetition-free finite-rank semantics of EC-T0008, with nonempty defending branches and deadness stopping play.

The supporting geometric bundle is listed in the exact outputs below. `opposition_edge_mate` and `opposition_up_reply_exists` require only a two-file margin for their narrower geometric statements; the main four-ply and follow-up theorems require four. Nonempty reply proofs prevent a vacuous all-replies claim and exclude stalemate in the nonedge phase. Safety/liveness uses EC-T0010; strong-turn legal arrivals use EC-T0007. Pure geometry is symbolic with `omega`, not class enumeration.

**Limit:** this is one useful contraction in the stated family, not a complete repeatable strategy or general KRK WDL. Opposition need not survive: Ka2/Rh3/Kc4 is a legal phase image. The suggested tighter image bounds and a terminating setup/re-entry policy are future obligations, not additions to this flagship statement. EC-C011 remains PROPOSED. No clocks, repetition, distance-to-mate theorem or initial-position claim follows.
