## Statement and scope

In the general FIDE 2023 rules kernel, let `q` contain exactly a king and rook of colour `σ` and the opposing king (`IsKRK q σ sk rs wk`). Both orientations and arbitrary castling/en-passant flags are included.

1. **Strong side to move:** if `q` was produced by a kernel-legal move and `q.turn = σ`, then `q` is not dead: some finite series of legal moves reaches a checkmate. The arisen-position corollary follows. A stronger helper needs only that the lone king is not in check rather than a legal-arrival hypothesis.
2. **Lone king to move:** if `q.turn = σ.opp`, then `q` is dead **iff** it is not already checkmate and every legal move leaves K vs K (captures the rook). The universal condition includes stalemate. This declaration needs no legal-arrival or global-reachability hypothesis.
3. **Rule corollary:** for an arisen K+R vs K position, `Rule5_2_2 q .draw` iff the lone king is to move, it is not checkmate and every legal move captures the rook.

`DeadK q` means no kernel-reachable position is checkmate. `MatedK` checks the side to move and absence of legal successors. `Rule5_2_2` is a named normative encoding of Article 5.2.2, not a derived theorem about what the Laws say.

## Proof and relation to EC-T0006

The internal KRK geometric move model is linked to the general kernel by proved soundness lemmas (`step_sound`, `mate_sound`). Reflections normalise the strong king. The rook relocation invariant and explicit cooperative replies move the rook to its home square; connectivity, settling and end-game geometry then produce a mate. The construction uses symbolic coordinates, not per-position enumeration.

With the lone king to move, a non-capturing legal move produces a strong-to-move KRK successor, hence a cooperative mate remains possible. Conversely, not mate and every successor K vs K imply deadness by **EC-T0006** (`krk_dead_of_forced_capture`, via EC-T0003). Thus EC-T0007 completes EC-T0006's converse and **freezes K+R vs K layer L1**.

This is existential cooperative reachability. It does not show that mate is forced against every defence, settle L2/WDL, provide a distance bound, or model clock/repetition draw rules. An optional rook capture must be handled separately in future optimal-play results.
