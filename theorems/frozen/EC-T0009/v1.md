# EC-T0009 v1 — Protected rook rank confinement against every legal reply

**Status:** FROZEN (S025, 2026-10-19). Source candidate: EC-C010.
**Representative ledger declaration:** `EndgameChess.Rules.krk_protected_rank_reply`.
**Attribution:** classical rook/king geometry; no novelty claim.

## Statement and exact scope

Let q contain exactly a king S and rook R of colour σ and the other colour's lone king W, on distinct squares (`IsKRK q σ S R W`), with the lone king to move. Squares are `(file, rank)` in 0…7. Assume

- `dInf S R = 1` (the strong king protects the rook);
- `S.rank < R.rank < W.rank` (the king is strictly below the rook, the lone king strictly above).

Then **every kernel-legal successor** r still has that same king and rook, its lone king on some square T, and the strong side to move. The actual reply is represented by `StepM.lone` and satisfies `ProtectedRank S R T`: the lone king remains above the rook rank and the rook survives. Both colours and any castling/en-passant flags are included; no global reachability/last-move hypothesis is needed.

The bundle also proves:

1. `krk_protected_reply`: if the rook is king-protected, every legal lone-king reply is covered by the non-capturing KRK model. This is a **completeness** direction beginning with arbitrary kernel `Legal`, not an assumption that the cooperative model's soundness covers all replies.
2. `rook_rank_barrier`: a one-square safe non-capturing step cannot cross the rook rank when the strong king is strictly below it.
3. `protected_rank_raise_unprotected`: keeping the king below the old rank and raising the rook to a higher rank puts it at Chebyshev distance at least two from its king. The immediate-protection invariant therefore does not by itself give an advancing strategy.

No legal reply is asserted to exist. A protected-rank stalemate is a real witness. There is no strict descent, closed full-game strategy invariant, setup theorem or general forced-mate conclusion.

## Proof, sources and examples

- Paper: `proofs/EC-C010_KRK_CONFINEMENT.md`; hostile audit: `audits/EC-C009_C010_PAPER.md`; formal fidelity audit: `audits/EC-C009_C010_LEAN.md`.
- Frozen primary source: `formal/EndgameChess/Rules/KRKConfinement.lean`, including the shared `ProtectedRank` definition and all four declarations above.
- The source imports the frozen general kernel and KRK geometry. A legal weak move must be a one-square king move: lone-side castling is excluded by absence of a lone-side rook. Post-move self-check forbids capture of the protected rook. A crossing would land on the rook rank; the strictly lower king cannot block its ray, contradicting self-check safety. Proofs are symbolic in the coordinates.
- `krk_protected_reply` and `krk_protected_rank_reply` use `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`. The two pure geometric lemmas `rook_rank_barrier` and `protected_rank_raise_unprotected` use `[propext, Quot.sound]`.
- Supporting fixed witnesses in `Rules/KRKForcingExamples.lean` show protected-rank stalemate (Kc6/Rb7/Ka8, Black to move) and failure of the non-strict king-rank hypothesis (Ke4/Rd4/Kg5 permits Kg4). The stalemate forcing check uses EC-T0008; the core confinement theorem has no dependency on a full forcing strategy. `decide` is limited to fixed examples, not the general proof.
