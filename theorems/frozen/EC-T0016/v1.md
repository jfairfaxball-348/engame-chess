## Exact scope and semantic boundary

The flagship assumes ONLY exact strong-turn Matches and strongRank<rookRank<weakRank. It concludes ForceMate σ q with no LowerExitWins, arrival, initial safety or file-margin hypothesis. A threatened rook is repaired before any weak turn. Nested induction on 7−rookRank and rookRank−strongRank discharges the pursuit exits. Every PhaseImage is included. The direct vertical-entry corollary additionally assumes weakRank≥strongRank+2, rook file≠strong file and rook/weak file margin≥2. Nonempty replies and ALL defences are proved. Fixed two-file-edge, former chase-cycle, threatened-rook forcing and naive setup/capture controls are supplementary, not a proof by examples.

Coordinates are Fin 8. Both named strong colours and arbitrary flags are quantified except where source noRights is explicitly required. Matches fixes exact board/material and turn; Legal is the general FIDE 2023 legal-move kernel. Quantifier order and all subsidiary hypotheses are authoritative in the verbatim exact-source statements below; no global initial-position reachability is added.

ForceMate is the unchanged EC-T0008 clock-free finite-rank named-colour semantics: actual opponent mate is terminal; strong choices are existential; defending legal replies are nonempty and universal; deadness stops play. A finite rank is not an optimal numeric distance. No halfmove clock, repetition, 50/75-move outcome, DTZ, complete FIDE WDL, seven-piece parity or initial-position conclusion is asserted.
