## Exact statement and scope

For **every** exact K+Q versus K board of either colour and turn satisfying
`KQK.FreshLegal`: distinct pieces, separated kings, the side which just
moved not in check, no castling rights or en-passant target; start at
clock zero with no incoming history. No initial-position reachability
trace is required.

The queen side has an independent actual bounded winning policy iff:
its turn, or the weak king is mated, or the queen is safe and a legal reply
exists. Safe means queen/weak-king distance at least two or king protection
at distance one. The weak side never has an actual winning policy. Both
sides have actual holding policies exactly when this criterion fails.

Strong-turn wins have a sufficient **97-ply** bound; all winning weak
turns fit **98 plies**, with already-mated roots at zero. These are fresh
rule-layer bounds, not just board bounds. No cursed wins/blessed losses
occur. Deadness is separate: weak turn, not mated, and every legal reply
captures the queen into KK. A drawn board may remain live.

The fresh layer preserves future current/intended draw claims, actual
pawn/capture clock resets, normalised repetition keys, terminal deadness,
stalemate and mate. Decreasing strategy rank prevents repetition and the
sub100 bound prevents a 50-move claim. Winning is equivalent to an
independent legal-policy definition with terminal safety and a uniform
horizon. Holding is defined independently on all compliant prefixes.

Main companion declarations: `KQK.strong_entry_bound`,
`KQK.safe_weak_entry_bound`, `KQK.fresh_strong97`, `KQK.fresh_weak98`,
`KQK.fresh_uniform98`, `KQK.fresh_force_iff`, `KQK.fresh_dead_iff`,
`FreshGame.fresh_sub100`, `FreshGame.actual_win_iff_rule` and
`FreshGame.hold_not_win` (all prefixed `EndgameChess.Rules`).
