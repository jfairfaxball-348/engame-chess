# Thirteen/fourteen-ply original-history criteria (EC-T0049 supporting bundle)

Certification update (2026-10-29): checked results are included in EC-T0049; see the [bundle scope and proof correspondence](KRK_BOUNDED_FRONTIER_CERTIFICATION.md). Any local-build counts or unfrozen/uncommitted statements below describe the pre-certification research checkpoint, not current publication status. Mathematical open obligations remain open.

For every ongoing RAW KRK history, either colour and all native flags, `KRKThirteenPlyHistory` gives an actual winning/holding iff at strong clock h>=86, and `KRKFourteenPlyHistory` gives the corresponding weak-turn iff at h>=85. Incoming traces are retained throughout; current and intended repetition/clock claims are tested on the original appended histories.

The strong criterion is an immediate mate or an ordinary king/rook entry into the complete twelve-ply region, together with the existing twelve-ply history certificate on that specified move. `KRKThirteenSlots` uses the sixty-four fixed coordinate points of the board, and rook rays are fixed axis intersections with `KRKTwelveGlobal.Region`; it contains no stored outcomes. This catalogue deliberately imposes no additional rank-bound lemma on the newly available deep regions. The weak criterion requires safe-rook, the claim bounds, and the strong certificate at every guarded reply.

`KRKCastleTwelve.castle_not_twelve` excludes both actual native castling directions, both colours, as nonmating twelve-ply entries. The argument first excludes every legal interior eleven-ply reply for the two castled king/rook arrangements. A shared boundary-pair argument covers their frame images. Covering the remaining inward replies confines a possible post-castling twelve-ply win to the home-rank corner on the queen side; that square held the original rook, contradicting the source KRK material. King-side castling is excluded directly. Rights and flags remain in the actual legal-move hypotheses.

The high-clock deadline then reduces any winning child to twelve plies. The strong iff splits an immediate terminal child from an ongoing child, applying the castling exclusion and preserving its exact appended trace. The weak iff retains the current/intended claim tests and all legal replies, including protected-rook cases.

`KRKThirteenPlyExact.rule_thirteen_iff` and `KRKFourteenPlyExact.rule_fourteen_iff` are unrestricted bounded-certificate iff theorems. The high-clock winners have exact rule distances in 1,3,5,7,9,11,13 on the strong turn, or 2,4,6,8,10,12,14 on the weak turn. Earlier-distance cases use the prior exact theorem; the new lower bounds use parity to reduce twelve to eleven, or thirteen to twelve. Thus displayed upper bounds are not asserted to be exact without the matching exclusions.

Near strip, inward corners and edge remain open. The canonical near thirteen-ply profile is complete in `KRKThirteenNearStrip`; the inward thirteen-ply ray converse remains partial.
